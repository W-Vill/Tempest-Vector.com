KEY FEATURES

2D Bullet Hell Roguelite

Engage in high intensity space combat that tests your piloting skills against swarms of enemy ships.

Vector-Thruster Flight Control

Master momentum-based space movement with precise drifting and physics-driven control.

Randomized Arsenal System

Your weapon cycles randomly from your full arsenal whenever ammo is depleted. Adapt or perish.

Upgrade System

Choose the right upgrades for your ship to suit your needs and survive the void.

Challenging Boss Encounters

Face difficult bosses that test reflexes, positioning, and combat skill.
